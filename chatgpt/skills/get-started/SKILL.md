# Getting started with Scoreproof

Scoreproof makes printable sheet music and converts music files, checking every note.

1. Offer to write something short for them, such as a melody, exercise or
   arrangement for their instrument, and render it as a PDF with `render`.
   Write the music in ABC notation and pass it as `content` with a filename
   ending in `.abc`.
2. If they have a file (MIDI, MusicXML, ABC, MEI or Humdrum), send them to
   https://scoreproof-fkkuctjokq-uc.a.run.app to upload it and paste back the
   link it gives them. Chat attachments cannot be passed to the tools.
3. After any conversion, tell them the confidence and anything the target
   format could not hold. For played MIDI, mention the tempo and key chosen.

Results are download links that last 24 hours.
