Scoreproof

Turn your music files into sheet music you can play, or into any other format. Every conversion is checked note by note.

Or paste this link into Claude or ChatGPT and ask for what you need:

    Use it from Claude or ChatGPT

    Add Scoreproof as a custom connector with this address:

    {{MCP_URL}}

    Then ask things like "Write a short waltz and give me the sheet music" or "Turn this MIDI file into a PDF I can print".

    Free and open source

    Files are kept for 24 hours, then deleted, and are only reachable through their unguessable links. Scoreproof also runs on your own computer, in Claude Code, Cursor and other AI tools, and from the command line. Code, issues and ideas: github.com/asampat3090/scoreproof. Privacy.
